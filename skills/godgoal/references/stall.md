# Stall and overhaul

_Read this when a stall trigger fires, or before claiming IMPOSSIBLE._

A stall is mechanical, not a mood. Any one triggers it:

- the same check and the same output twice;
- three passes with no case, check or theory moving;
- three fix rounds with no check changing state;
- a metric that has not improved in three recorded experiments beyond noise;
- three failed hypotheses in a debug run.

The response is **overhaul**, not a stop and not the same attempt again. You
may first run a short divergent panel (two or three fresh read-only agents,
each told to take a different lens: invert the requirement, remove the
abstraction, move a layer, question the design, check prior art, reproduce
fresh, diff two states, relax a self-imposed constraint, instrument the
boundary). Each returns a reframing, a falsifiable theory, the cheapest probe,
what would kill it, and the cost if wrong. The panel is recon, not an ending.

Overhaul:

1. Freeze the goal. Snapshot evidence and tombstones.
2. Retire the current plan, theories and assumptions from the working set.
   The old model is dead, not hidden.
3. Re-derive the edge cases from the goal. Add the ones the old approach could
   not see. Reopen the landscape where the old theory narrowed it.
4. Generate at least two rivals with different predictions on the cheapest
   discriminating case.
5. Probe that case. Save the result.
6. Dispatch fresh agents with the goal, the evidence, the tombstones, the new
   edge cases and the rival pair. No old plan, no build history.
7. Fresh critic, then repeat. The run stays `RUNNING` across contexts.

A stall clears only after a new pass with a different result, or a completed
overhaul. A repeated identical failure, or a ruling, does not clear it.
Two panel rounds with nothing decisive is the trigger for the overhaul, not
an ending. Before any claim that the goal is impossible, at least one
completed overhaul must be on record.
